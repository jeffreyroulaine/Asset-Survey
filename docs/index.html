📲 Add to Home Screen

Follow these steps in Safari on your iPhone or iPad to install Asset Survey as an app on your home screen:

1
Open this page in Safari (not Chrome or another browser)
2
Tap the Share button ↑ at the bottom of the screen
3
Scroll down and tap "Add to Home Screen"
4
Tap "Add" in the top right corner
✅ The app icon will appear on your home screen. It opens full-screen with no browser chrome — just like a native app.
